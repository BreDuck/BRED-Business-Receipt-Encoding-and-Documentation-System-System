# Receipt-Management-System

### Overview

The **Receipt Management System** is a centralized application designed to record, organize, monitor, and manage business receipts and purchase transactions. It allows users to encode receipt information and maintain a digital archive of all recorded transactions, making it easier to retrieve, review, and track financial records.

Each receipt is stored in a database and displayed in a searchable receipt directory. Users can click on a specific receipt number to view its complete details in a structured format resembling the original physical receipt.

### Key Features

- **Receipt Management** – Add, view, edit, delete, and organize encoded receipts.
- **Detailed Receipt Records** – View receipt numbers, transaction dates, TIN numbers, supplier information, purchased items, quantities, unit prices, and total amounts.
- **Product and Item Tracking** – Record individual products purchased, including their descriptions, quantities, prices, and subtotals.
- **Supplier Management** – Maintain supplier information, including business names, addresses, contact details, and TIN numbers.
- **Project Assignment** – Associate receipts with specific projects to monitor project-related purchases and expenses.
- **Search and Filtering** – Find receipts using receipt numbers, TIN numbers, supplier names, product descriptions, dates, or project names.
- **Receipt Attachments** – Upload and store scanned copies, photographs, or PDF versions of original receipts.
- **Expense Monitoring** – Track total purchases and expenses across different projects and suppliers.
- **Reports and Analytics** – Generate expense summaries and transaction reports based on dates, projects, suppliers, and other criteria.
- **User Management** – Manage user accounts, roles, and access permissions.
- **Activity Logs** – Track receipt encoding, modifications, and other system activities.

### Receipt Information

Each encoded receipt can contain the following information:

| Category | Information |
|---|---|
| Receipt Details | Receipt Number, Receipt Date, Receipt Type |
| Supplier Details | Business Name, TIN, Address, Contact Number |
| Purchase Details | Item Description, Quantity, Unit Price, Subtotal |
| Financial Details | VAT, Discounts, Additional Charges, Grand Total |
| Project Details | Project Name, Project Code, Fund Source |
| Attachments | Original Receipt Image or PDF |
| Record Details | Encoded By, Date Created, Last Modified |

### Main Objective

The primary objective of this project is to provide an organized and accessible digital recordkeeping system for business receipts. Rather than manually searching through physical receipts and documents, users can quickly locate transactions and access their complete purchase information through a centralized platform.

The system aims to improve receipt management, transaction tracking, project expense monitoring, document organization, and financial record accessibility.
